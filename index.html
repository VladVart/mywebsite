Our Story

It all began with a small plot of land and a big dream: to make wine we are proud of and love to share. We grew up among the vines and we know that good grapes are born where they are loved.

Today we still harvest by hand, carefully select every cluster and give each wine the time it needs to mature. No rush, only patience, hard work and a little bit of magic.
